Questions, answered.

A few things worth knowing before you book your tune.

How accurate are the gains shown?

The figures shown are a rough close estimate based on typical tuning results for the selected vehicle. Actual gains can vary depending on vehicle condition, fuel, modifications, ECU software and tuning setup.

Will my car get the exact figure shown?

Not necessarily. Every vehicle is different, so the final result can vary. We always recommend treating calculator figures as an indication rather than a guaranteed dyno result.

Can you tune modified vehicles?

Yes. Modified vehicles can be assessed individually so the calibration can be matched to the hardware and the owner's goals.

What stage should I choose?

That depends on your vehicle, hardware and what you want from it. If you're unsure, get in touch and we'll help you choose the most suitable option.
